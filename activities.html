Diving & Snorkeling ⭐ 4.7 · #2 Water Sport in Freeport

Snorkeling & Scuba Diving at UNEXSO

Grand Bahama's crystal-clear waters are home to vibrant coral reefs, tropical fish, and incredible marine life. UNEXSO — established since 1965 — is the premier dive and snorkel operator in the Bahamas, offering shark dives, snorkel sea safaris, glass-bottom boat tours, and unforgettable dolphin encounters at Sanctuary Bay. Rated 4.7 stars by over 1,000 visitors.

Port Lucaya, Freeport Daily 8am–6pm +1 800-992-3483 sanctuarybaybahamas.com

What's Available

  • 🤿 Snorkel sea safari tours
  • 🦈 Shark diving & reef dives
  • 🐬 Dolphin encounters at Sanctuary Bay
  • 🚤 Glass-bottom boat tours
  • 🌊 Sightseeing boat tours
  • 🎓 Education & research programs

Deep Sea Fishing

Charter a boat and head out to the deep blue for mahi-mahi, wahoo, tuna, and marlin. Grand Bahama's waters are among the most productive fishing grounds in the Atlantic. Multiple charter operators depart from Freeport Harbour daily.

Freeport Harbour Half/Full Day trips Year-round

What's Available

  • 🐟 Mahi-mahi & wahoo
  • 🐠 Yellowfin tuna
  • 🦈 Deep sea marlin
  • 🚤 Private charter boats
  • 🎣 All gear provided
  • 🏆 Tournament fishing available

Wildlife Experience ⭐ 4.7 · World-Famous Experience

Swim with Dolphins

One of the most magical experiences in the Bahamas — swim alongside Atlantic bottlenose dolphins in the open water at UNEXSO's Sanctuary Bay. Guided by professional marine trainers, this bucket-list encounter is suitable for all ages and skill levels.

Sanctuary Bay, UNEXSO Daily 8am–6pm +1 800-992-3483 sanctuarybaybahamas.com

Highlights

  • 🐬 Open-water dolphin swim
  • 🌊 Sanctuary Bay facility
  • 👨‍🏫 Pro marine trainers
  • 🎓 Educational programs
  • 📸 Photos available
  • 🏆 Bucket-list experience
Golf ⭐ 3.4 · Only 18-Hole Course on Grand Bahama

Golf at Reef Golf Course

Grand Bahama's only 18-hole resort golf course, designed by the legendary Robert Trent Jones Jr. Spanning 6,909 yards with bunkers, water hazards, and lush fairways — once named one of the world's Top 100 golf courses. Features a pro shop, on-site restaurant, and stunning tropical surroundings.

Seahorse Road, Freeport Advance booking recommended Green fees from $85 Cart rental required

Highlights

  • ⛳ 18-hole championship course
  • 🏆 Robert Trent Jones Jr. design
  • 🛒 Pro shop on-site
  • 🍽️ On-site restaurant
  • 🌴 Tropical surroundings
  • 🚗 Powered cart included

Beach ⭐ 4.5 · #2 on Grand Bahama Island

Gold Rock Beach

Frequently listed among the most beautiful beaches in the world. Part of Lucayan National Park, Gold Rock Beach features dramatic low-tide sandbars, crystal-clear turquoise water, and powdery white sand in total seclusion. A filming location for Pirates of the Caribbean.

Lucayan National Park, East Grand Bahama Daily access Adults $11, Children $6 +1 242-352-5438

Highlights

  • 🏖️ World-class white sand beach
  • 🌊 Crystal-clear turquoise water
  • ⭐ Filming location - Pirates of the Caribbean
  • 🦀 Starfish & shells at low tide
  • 🌴 Total seclusion & tranquility
  • 🐚 Limestone cave system nearby
National Park ⭐ 4.5 · #2 on Grand Bahama Island

Lucayan National Park

Explore one of the world's longest charted underwater cave systems at Lucayan National Park. With 40 acres of diverse ecosystems including Ben's Cave, mangrove boardwalks, and the stunning Gold Rock Beach, this park is a must-visit natural treasure.

Grand Bahama Highway (25 miles east of Freeport) Daily access Adults $11, Children $6 +1 242-352-5438

Highlights

  • 🗺️ World's longest underwater cave system
  • 🌊 Gold Rock Beach access
  • 🌿 Mangrove boardwalk trails
  • 🐚 Ben's Cave & Burial Mound Cave
  • 🦀 Marine life & starfish
  • 🎬 Pirates of the Caribbean filming location
