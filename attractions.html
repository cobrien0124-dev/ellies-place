Beach ⭐ 4.5 · #2 on Grand Bahama Island

Gold Rock Beach

Frequently listed among the most beautiful beaches in the world. Part of Lucayan National Park, Gold Rock Beach features dramatic low-tide sandbars, crystal-clear turquoise water, and powdery white sand in total seclusion. A filming location for Pirates of the Caribbean.

Lucayan National Park, East Grand Bahama Daily access Adults $11, Children $6 +1 242-352-5438

Highlights

  • 🏖️ World-class white sand beach
  • 🌊 Crystal-clear turquoise water
  • ⭐ Filming location - Pirates of the Caribbean
  • 🦀 Starfish & shells at low tide
  • 🌴 Total seclusion & tranquility
  • 🐚 Limestone cave system nearby
Shopping & Entertainment

Port Lucaya Marketplace

The social heart of Freeport! Port Lucaya Marketplace is a vibrant waterfront hub packed with boutique shops, restaurants, bars, and live entertainment. By day it's great for shopping souvenirs and local crafts; by night it comes alive with music at Count Basie Square, dancing, and the energy of locals and visitors mixing in a true Caribbean atmosphere.

Port Lucaya, Freeport Open Daily Live Music Nightly

What's There

  • 🛍️ Boutique shops & souvenir stalls
  • 🍽️ 10+ restaurants & bars
  • 🎵 Count Basie Square live music
  • 🎨 Local arts & craft vendors
  • ⛵ Marina & waterfront views
  • 🌙 Best nightlife hub in Freeport
National Park ⭐ 4.5 · #2 on Grand Bahama Island

Lucayan National Park

Explore one of the world's longest charted underwater cave systems at Lucayan National Park. With 40 acres of diverse ecosystems including Ben's Cave, mangrove boardwalks, and the stunning Gold Rock Beach, this park is a must-visit natural treasure.

Grand Bahama Highway (25 miles east of Freeport) Daily access Adults $11, Children $6 +1 242-352-5438

Highlights

  • 🗺️ World's longest underwater cave system
  • 🌊 Gold Rock Beach access
  • 🌿 Mangrove boardwalk trails
  • 🐚 Ben's Cave & Burial Mound Cave
  • 🦀 Marine life & starfish
  • 🎬 Pirates of the Caribbean filming location
Botanical Garden ⭐ 4.1 · #1 Attraction in Freeport

Garden of the Groves

A stunning 12-acre tropical paradise ranked the #1 attraction in Freeport on TripAdvisor with over 1,000 reviews! Wander lush pathways through native Bahamian flora, spot birds, butterflies, turtles and ducks, marvel at the waterfall, explore the labyrinth, visit the charming chapel, and finish with cocktails at the Garden Café. Guided tours available.

Magellan & Midshipman Dr, Freeport Daily 9am–4pm $17 per person +1 242-374-7778

Highlights

  • 🦋 Birds, butterflies, turtles & ducks
  • 💧 Waterfall & walking paths
  • 🌀 Labyrinth & meditation garden
  • ⛪ Charming on-site chapel
  • 🍹 Garden Café & cocktail bar
  • 🛍️ Straw market & souvenir shops
Diving & Water Sports ⭐ 4.7 · #2 Water Sport in Freeport

UNEXSO — Underwater Explorer's Society

Established in 1965, UNEXSO is the most celebrated dive & marine experience center in the Bahamas, rated 4.7 stars across 1,000+ reviews. Offering glass-bottom boat tours, snorkel sea safaris, shark dives, and world-famous dolphin encounters at Sanctuary Bay. Staffed by knowledgeable, kind, and entertaining professionals — a truly bucket-list experience.

Port Lucaya, Freeport Daily 8am–6pm +1 800-992-3483 sanctuarybaybahamas.com

Experiences

  • 🐬 Dolphin encounters at Sanctuary Bay
  • 🦈 Shark diving & reef dives
  • 🤿 Snorkel sea safari tours
  • 🚤 Glass-bottom boat tours
  • 🌊 Sightseeing boat tours
  • 🎓 Education & research programs
Beach ⭐ 4.1 · #2 Thing to Do in Freeport

Taino Beach

One of Freeport's most beloved beaches — a stretch of powdery white sand with calm, clear turquoise water perfect for families. Taino Beach is lined with beach bars, restaurants, and water sport rentals, and offers stunning sunsets over the water.

30 Jolly Roger Drive, Lucaya Open daily · Free access Water sports available

Highlights

  • 🏖️ Powdery white sand
  • 🌊 Calm family-friendly waters
  • 🛥️ Jet ski & parasailing
  • 🏄 Paddleboard rentals
  • 🍹 Beach bars & restaurants
  • 🌅 Beautiful sunsets
Nature & Wildlife

Rand Nature Centre

A 100-acre wildlife reserve and arboretum operated by the Bahamas National Trust. Walk nature trails through native pineland, spot tropical birds, turtles, and the endangered Bahamas parrot. Features an indoor museum, art gallery, and guided bird-watching tours.

E. Settlers Way, Freeport Guided tours Tue & Thu 10:30am Adults $12, Children $6 +1 242-352-5438

Highlights

  • 🦩 West Indian flamingos
  • 🦜 Endangered Bahamas parrot
  • 🌿 100-acre nature trails
  • 🐢 Turtle & bird pond
  • 🏛️ Indoor museum & art gallery
  • 🔭 Bird-watching tours
Island & Marine Park ⭐ 4.5 · #7 Thing to Do in Freeport

Peterson Cay National Park

A secluded 1.5-acre island paradise and the only cay on Grand Bahama's south coast — accessible by boat only. Surrounded by 1,000 acres of protected marine habitat, Peterson Cay offers world-class snorkeling, vibrant coral reefs, sea turtles, and total seclusion just one mile offshore.

Off southern coast, Freeport Boat access only Tour packages from $65 +1 242-393-1317

Highlights

  • 🐢 Sea turtles & nurse sharks
  • 🐠 Vibrant coral reefs
  • 🤿 World-class snorkeling
  • 🏝️ Private secluded island
  • 🦞 Lobsters & starfish
  • 🧺 Perfect for picnics & BBQ
Shopping & Culture

Freeport City Markets & Straw Market

Browse authentic handcrafted Bahamian goods made by local artisans — straw bags, wood carvings, painted artwork, and island jewellery. The Straw Market is the perfect place to find unique, meaningful souvenirs that support local craftspeople.

Downtown Freeport Daily 9am–5pm Free entry

Highlights

  • 🧺 Handwoven straw goods
  • 🎨 Local artwork & paintings
  • 🪵 Wood carvings & sculptures
  • 💍 Island jewellery
  • 🛍️ Authentic Bahamian souvenirs
  • 👩‍🎨 Meet local artisans
