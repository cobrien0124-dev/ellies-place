Greek-Bahamian Seafood

Zorba's Restaurant

A Freeport institution and family favourite since the 1970s! Located right in Port Lucaya Marketplace, Zorba's blends authentic Greek recipes with the freshest local Bahamian seafood. Known for their legendary cracked conch, conch chowder, coconut shrimp, and Greek-inspired dishes served in a relaxed, welcoming atmosphere.

Port Lucaya Marketplace $$ Dine-in & Takeout

Fan Favourites

  • 🍲 Conch Chowder & Conch Stew
  • 🍤 Coconut Shrimp with Rice
  • 🐟 Fresh Local Fish
  • 🥗 Greek Salad
  • 🍮 Homemade Desserts

Authentic Bahamian

Becky's Restaurant

A true Freeport institution beloved by locals for decades. Becky's serves authentic Out-Islands Bahamian cuisine — minced lobster, curried mutton, fish platters, baked chicken, conch salad, and legendary all-day Bahamian breakfasts. Away from the tourist strip, this is where locals eat.

Sunrise Highway & East Beach Drive, Freeport $ Budget +1 242-352-5247

Local Favourites

  • 🦞 Minced lobster
  • 🐑 Curried mutton
  • 🐟 Fresh fish platters
  • 🍳 All-day Bahamian breakfast
  • 🐚 Fresh conch salad
  • 🍗 Baked & curried chicken
Fine Dining / Steakhouse

Churchill's Chophouse

Freeport's most elegant dining experience, set within the Grand Lucayan resort with old-world English charm. Featuring a wood piano bar, chandeliers, and murals depicting Bahamian life, Churchill's is renowned for its signature Prime Rib, lobster risotto, and veal marsala. Reservations required.

Grand Lucayan Resort, Sea Horse Road Thu–Sat 6pm–10pm $$$ Fine Dining +1 242-373-1333

Signature Experience

  • 🥩 Signature Prime Rib
  • 🦞 Lobster & wild mushroom risotto
  • 🎹 Live piano bar
  • 🍝 Customizable pasta
  • 🍷 Extensive wine list
  • 👔 Smart casual dress required

Rum Runners

An iconic Freeport landmark with walls covered in dollar bills from around the world! Rum Runners at Port Lucaya Marketplace is famous for its strong tropical cocktails, happy hour BOGO deals, and laid-back island atmosphere. The perfect spot to kick off your evening.

Sea Horse Road, Port Lucaya Marketplace Daily 9am–midnight $ +1 242-373-7233

Why You'll Love It

  • 🎉 Happy hour BOGO 4–7pm
  • 🍗 $1 wings & conch fritters
  • 💵 Famous dollar-bill covered walls

Local Favorite

Conch World

The freshest conch salad in the Bahamas — prepared right in front of you! Watch as skilled hands crack, clean, and season fresh conch with onions, peppers, citrus, and hot sauce. A quintessential Bahamian street food experience you absolutely cannot miss.

Freeport Area $ Must Try!

Pisces Restaurant

A Freeport favourite since 1980! Pisces is a dockside gem at the Grand Bahama Yacht Club marina, known for its legendary King Neptune Pizza loaded with lobster, crab, and shrimp. Enjoy outdoor covered seating overlooking the marina with a full bar and late-night dining.

Grand Bahama Yacht Club, Midshipman Rd, Port Lucaya Mon–Sat 4pm–midnight $$ +1 242-373-5192

Must-Try Dishes

  • 🍕 King Neptune Pizza (lobster/crab/shrimp)
  • 🐠 Conch pizza & seafood curry
  • 🦞 Lobster bacon mac & cheese
  • ⚓ Marina waterfront seating
  • 🌙 Late-night dining
  • 🥗 Vegetarian & vegan options
Local Experience

Local Fish Fry

The most authentic Bahamian dining experience you'll find! Every Friday and Saturday evening, Freeport locals gather for the Fish Fry — grilled snapper, cracked conch, macaroni salad, peas & rice, coleslaw, and cold Kalik beers. This is the real Bahamas.

Midway Shopping Center, Freeport Fri & Sat evenings $ Budget

On the Grill

  • 🐟 Grilled fresh snapper
  • 🐚 Cracked conch
  • 🍚 Peas & rice
  • 🥗 Macaroni salad
  • 🍺 Cold Kalik beer
  • 🇧🇸 100% authentic Bahamian
Waterfront Fusion ⭐ 4.0 · #14 Restaurant in Freeport

Sabor Restaurant

A hidden waterfront treasure set in the gardens of Pelican Bay Hotel at Port Lucaya Marina. Sabor blends Caribbean, Bahamian, and international flavours under swaying palm trees with stunning yacht marina views. Known for Thai coconut curry seafood and lobster mango pasta.

Bahamian Seafood
