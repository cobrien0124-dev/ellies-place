Nightlife

Live music, cocktails, dancing, and unforgettable Bahamian nights

After Dark

Best Nightlife in Freeport

When the sun goes down, the fun is just getting started in Grand Bahama

Live Music ⭐ Top Nightlife Spot

Count Basie Square

The heartbeat of Freeport nightlife. This iconic open-air stage at Port Lucaya Marketplace comes alive nightly with live Bahamian bands, rake & scrape music, goombay drumming, and impromptu dancing. Named after the jazz legend who lived in the Bahamas, this is where locals and visitors come together under the stars.

Port Lucaya Marketplace, Sea Horse Road Nightly from 9pm Free admission

What to Expect

  • 🎶 Live Bahamian bands nightly
  • 🥁 Rake & scrape folk music
  • 💃 Open-air dancing
  • 🍹 Bars & restaurants surround the square
  • 🌟 Free admission
  • 🌴 Tropical open-air atmosphere
Bar & Lounge ⭐ 4.4 · Icon of Port Lucaya

Rum Runners

Freeport's most iconic bar — walls literally covered in dollar bills from around the world. A Port Lucaya institution since the '80s, Rum Runners is famous for its impossibly strong tropical cocktails, BOGO happy hour, and legendary Liquid Marijuana. The perfect place to start (or end) your night.

Port Lucaya Marketplace Daily 9am–midnight $ · Happy hour 4–7pm

Signature Drinks

  • 🍹 Rum Runner (the original)
  • 🌿 Liquid Marijuana
  • 🍦 Miami Vice
  • 🎉 BOGO Happy Hour 4–7pm
  • 💵 Famous dollar-bill walls
  • 🌊 Waterfront outdoor seating
Beach Bar ⭐ Local Favourite

Taino Beach Bar

A laid-back paradise where you can sip cocktails with your toes in the white sand. Taino Beach's bar scene comes alive on weekends with live music, cool cocktails, and the most spectacular ocean views on the island. The ultimate chill-out spot.

Taino Beach, Grand Bahama Daily · Live music weekends $

Beach Bar Life

  • 🌊 Feet-in-the-sand drinking
  • 🎵 Live music on weekends
  • 🍹 Frozen cocktails & Kalik beers
  • 🌅 Spectacular ocean sunsets
  • 🐚 Fresh conch snacks nearby
  • 🏊 Swimming right off the beach
Cultural Festival 🇧🇸 Must-See Bahamian Tradition

Junkanoo Festival

The ultimate Bahamian celebration! Held annually on Boxing Day (Dec 26) and New Year's Day, Junkanoo transforms the streets with elaborate handcrafted costumes, goombay drums, cowbells, and dancers in a dazzling all-night parade. The Bahamas' most spectacular cultural event.

Downtown Freeport Dec 26 & Jan 1 · from midnight Free to watch

Junkanoo Experience

  • 🥁 Goombay drums & cowbells
  • 🎨 Elaborate handmade costumes
  • 💃 Street parade & dancing
  • 🌙 All-night celebration
  • 🏆 Competition between groups
  • 🇧🇸 Most iconic Bahamian tradition
